The defensible user benefit

Eligible existing-file edits can avoid full-file regeneration; task, model, context, isolated workspace, validation evidence, callback and manager decision stay in one repository-scoped loop; and failures remain visible instead of being rewritten as success. A universal Source Graph token multiplier and system-wide ROI remain unmeasured.
