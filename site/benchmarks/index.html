Measured, not claimed

Every number keeps its denominator and its limits.

AIWorkHub separates structural bytes, provider tokens, elapsed time, cost and accepted quality. The first semantic-edit pilot is published as preliminary evidence—not as a universal multiplier.

Why this is not a product claim

The sample contains only two non-randomized, non-order-balanced pairs from one model and one small task family. Cache composition differs, cost is unknown, explicit manager acceptance is absent and one focused run predates authenticated semantic-edit receipts. The uncached-input result moved against the other totals.

Structural telemetry is separate

Operations reports existing-file bytes, selected-region bytes, replacement bytes and bytes re-emitted by the model. That ratio describes output shape. It never becomes a token or cost estimate without a matched provider baseline.
