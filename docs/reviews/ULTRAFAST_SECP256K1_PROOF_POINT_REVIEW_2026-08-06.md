# UltrafastSecp256k1 proof-point review

Source: owner-supplied public-repository assessment, attachment

## Assessment

UltrafastSecp256k1 is a useful proof point for AIWorkHub's operating method:
reproducible assurance, scoped review profiles, claim traceability, benchmark
discipline, backend parity, continuous regression hardening, and reported
production adoption. It is evidence that a rigorous AI-assisted workflow can
produce reviewable high-stakes software; it is not proof that AIWorkHub
guarantees autonomous production quality.

## Safe product lesson

AIWorkHub should operationalize the controls needed to make AI-authored work
bounded, reproducible, reviewable, and continuously improvable. A future case
study may link public claims only to pinned upstream sources and reproducible
artifacts. Adoption, performance, and audit-count statements must be rechecked
against the donor repository at publication time.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Claim ledger with ID, scope, evidence, replay command, and status.
- Review profiles for tasking, Source Graph, extension, memory/context,
  reviewer/quality, and storage/callback surfaces.
- Reviewer quickstart and replayable evidence bundle.
- Finding/claim/invariant-to-test traceability.
- Residual-risk register and benchmark claim discipline.
- A control-plane failure catalog with permanent negative regressions.
