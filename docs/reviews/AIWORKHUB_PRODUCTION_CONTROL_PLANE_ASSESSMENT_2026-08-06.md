# Production control-plane assessment

Source: owner-supplied assessment, attachment
`b1057779-15d8-46fb-89cb-542045b2dd80`, received 2026-08-06.

## Assessment

AIWorkHub is evaluated as a serious local-first, repository-scoped,
multi-model engineering control plane rather than a chat wrapper. It replaces
manual multi-chat coordination with task authority, isolated workers, graph
context, durable knowledge surfaces, callback review, and fail-closed manager
acceptance.

The system's control-plane maturity is ahead of its fully autonomous quality
and economic optimization. Structural byte reductions, continuous Source Graph
use, and model-price differences must remain distinct from proven provider
token savings or causal quality gains.

## Priority recommendations

- Route-local circuit isolation for model timeouts and bridge failures.
- Meaningful-output and behavioral anti-collapse gates.
- Atomic callback and terminal-state persistence.
- Runtime wiring of quality adapters such as SARIF, JUnit, coverage, and
  benchmark evidence.
- Diagnostic delta rework instead of blind retry.
- Matched-denominator `cost_per_accepted_outcome` advisory routing.
- Characterization-first decomposition of large authority modules.

## Explicit non-goals

Do not introduce inferred default token caps, call structural byte ratios token
savings, rank unknown cost as free, or enable autonomous economic routing before
matched canaries establish outcome parity.
